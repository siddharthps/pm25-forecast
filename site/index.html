    How this forecast is made

    Each morning at 06:00 Chicago time, each monitor's forecast for tomorrow is its average PM2.5 over the last complete day ("persistence"), the standard baseline that better models must beat. The area's value is the highest monitor, converted to the Air Quality Index as set out in EPA's Technical Assistance Document ().

    Data and credits

    The air quality data come from the agencies that operate these monitors: Maricopa County Air Quality Department, Arizona Department of Environmental Quality and Pinal County Air Quality Control District, through the U.S. EPA AirNow program (AirNow partners).

    • EPA AirNow: the live, preliminary hourly data behind every forecast on this page.
    • EPA Air Quality System (AQS): validated history, used to build and test the forecasting models.
    • Open-Meteo (licensed CC BY 4.0): weather history and forecasts, being added as model inputs.
    • Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS): atmospheric composition forecasts, being added as a comparison.
